## 原题（英文原文）

```text
For clinical trial NCT02838420, review the more effective regimen ingredients and identify which ingredient starts with the letter C.

Then, determine which pharmaceutical company received the most recent FDA approval (up until December 2024) for this identified ingredient.
Note that we are only look for overall FDA drug approval, not new indidcation, not supplemental approvals.

If this company is listed on any US stock market (including over-the-counter markets), provide:
1. The stock ticker symbol
2. The opening stock price on the FDA approval date

If the company is not listed on any US stock market, please indicate "NOT LISTED".
```

## 本题怎样算答对

四组共用以下标准，判断的是最终答案；“正确”不等于每句话无误或安全指导完整。

1. 确认NCT02838420较有效方案中C开头的成分及截至2024年12月最近整体FDA批准公司和日期
2. 核实该公司在美国含OTC的上市情况、ticker及FDA批准日开盘价；未上市则NOT LISTED
3. 最终交付要求：对应FDA申请者的美国上市信息、股票代码及批准日开盘价；只有题设与来源支持时才使用NOT LISTED。

以上用于核对最终字段的事实对象与关系；原题只要求成分名或日期时，不要求在答卷中打印全部中间检索链。

核验来源：[依据1](<https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30981696/>)、[依据2](<https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02838420>)、[依据3](<https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shan23chen/MedBrowseComp/446e9b31372df271c13d2ee76f1b0db0e335ccd8/data/final_nct_121_with_effecacy_group.csv>)。全部来源保留于[题级标准](../answer_requirements_100.json)。
