## base

A readable, cautious synthesis with useful scope distinctions, but misstates statistical significance and incompletely describes the actual comparisons.

优点：

- Correctly treats collagen as a different dressing and broad moist-dressing evidence as insufficient to isolate hydrogel benefit; gives an appropriately uncertain conclusion.

具体问题：

- [moderate] The supplied MD0.40 interval0.28–0.52 excludes zero. Very-low-certainty clinical evidence is not equivalent to a nonsignificant statistical estimate.；支持证据ID：E1。
- [moderate] The source includes active dressing comparators and antiseptic versus non-antiseptic hydrogel, not an established no-dressing comparison. It omits the actual two-trial162-person scope.；支持证据ID：E2, E3。

## epoch1

Substantial incoherence, misbound citation and invented interpretation mean the answer does not reliably use the available hydrogel evidence.

优点：

- Mentions the two-trial review and the need to distinguish hydrogel from other moist dressings.

具体问题：

- [major] Unintelligible mixed-language fragments and unsupported healing-benefit framing prevent a reliable clinical interpretation of the review.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E3。
- [major] E10 contains only the heading Donor Site Infection and cannot support those numbers. E5 describes an 18-month study period, not patient follow-up.；支持证据ID：E5, E10。

## epoch1_5

Uses relevant review but distorts the comparator and adds unsupported statistical interpretation; the general uncertainty conclusion does not repair those errors.

优点：

- Recognizes low certainty and distinguishes collagen from hydrogel-specific evidence.

具体问题：

- [major] Substitutes an undressed/no-treatment comparison for the actual question about effectiveness and the supplied other-dressing comparators. E1 compares two hydrogels; E3 describes alginate/Cuticerin controls.；支持证据ID：E1, E3。
- [major] Invents a reason to discount the explicitly reported MD 0.40 and CI. No supplied passage says the CI is uninterpretable because a standard error is missing.；支持证据ID：E1。

## epoch2

Relevant structure and cautious language cannot compensate for an invented 22-day result at the center of the answer.

优点：

- Separates hydrogel, broader moist-dressing and collagen evidence, and does not equate uncertainty with ineffectiveness.

具体问题：

- [major] Fabricates a central effect estimate and changes mean difference into a median. E1 reports MD 0.40 (95% CI 0.28–0.52), not 22 days.；支持证据ID：E1。
- [major] Overfocuses on an undressed comparator that the review did not test, rather than directly explaining uncertain effectiveness versus other dressings.；支持证据ID：E3, E4。
- [moderate] Does not clearly distinguish the study recruitment period from healing follow-up; including collagen numbers adds distraction rather than hydrogel-specific support.；支持证据ID：E5。
