## base

优点：

- Correctly names MSBase disability models,Spider-MS and relapse/CDP Bayesian/fixed-effect frameworks with routine clinical inputs.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Items3and4duplicate the OFSEP replication;15240is total cohort rather than training set. E4figure caption does not establish high accuracy; E7reports miscalibration. Omits BRS/EDSS3and MSBase two-year/six-month target horizon.；支持证据ID：E1, E4, E5, E7, E9。
- [major] No calibration, external-validation or clinical-readiness cautions despite explicit source concerns and original Stühler black-box limitations.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E7, E9。
