## base

Thoughtful uncertainty cannot repair the central comparator error and misstatement of augmentation functional evidence.

优点：

- Recognizes lack of repair/no-repair evidence and higher xenograft complication risk without claiming a quantified recovery impact.

具体问题：

- [major] Early-versus-delayed rehabilitation after repair cannot establish repair-versus-no-repair benefit;opening asserts the missing comparison.；支持证据ID：E1, E2。
- [moderate] E9reports improvedConstant scores while no strength/ROM difference;allograft benefits inE11also warrant distinction. Blanket functional-null claim loses outcome specificity.；支持证据ID：E8, E9, E11, E12。

## epoch1

Good recognition of evidence mismatch but still claims repair benefit and misdescribes sources.

优点：

- Correctly recognizes postoperative rehabilitation comparisons cannot establish repair versus no-repair effectiveness.

具体问题：

- [major] No supplied repair-versus-no-repair comparison supports the opening; neither cited source is such a comparison and the search dates are invented.；支持证据ID：E1, E3, E7。
- [moderate] Omits the directly supplied increased xenograft complication odds despite discussing surgical options.；支持证据ID：E7, E12。

## epoch1_5

Material comparator and graft-type confusion prevents a dependable answer to whether repair works.

优点：

- Reports xenograft complicationOR3.65 and acknowledges lack of nonsurgical comparison.

具体问题：

- [major] All rehabilitation patients had repair; no decline of repair success is shown. E11 favors allograft, not xenograft.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E11。

## epoch2

优点：

- Separates augmentation from repair-versus-no-repair and mentions Constant-score finding.

- [major] Rehabilitation timing is not repair timing or proof of repair efficacy; one study retained15-month function difference. Gastroscopy and invented complications warning are irrelevant unsupported additions.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E7。
- [moderate] Invented warning displaces explicit higher xenograft complication rate.；支持证据ID：E7, E12。
