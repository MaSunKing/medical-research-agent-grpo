## base

Relevant but leaves major directly available context unused, giving insufficiently calibrated support to a favorable selected regimen.

优点：

- Correctly reports75% regimen subgroup and distinguishes STAR-ter planned protocol from results.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Omits the62-person retrospective design, overall16%12-month eradication, unknown subgroup size and untreated negative cultures. Selective highest rate creates an overpositive impression and cannot establish treatment-caused clearance.；支持证据ID：E1。

## epoch1

Some relevant results but study design/eligibility errors and excessive tissue-specific framing impair the answer.

优点：

- Reports overall16% and regimen75% eradication and recognizes STAR-ter has no supplied results.

具体问题：

- [major] E1 is retrospective chart review of first/recurrent-after-year incident infection, and the four-negative-culture group is included, not excluded.；支持证据ID：E1。
- [moderate] Adds an unnecessarily absolute tissue/permanence target and unsupported timing qualification, distracting from observed12-month eradication.；支持证据ID：E1, E4。

## epoch1_5

Excessive lung-tissue caveats, invented subgroup size and missing overall result weaken usefulness.

优点：

- Recognizes observational regimen signal and lack of STAR-ter results.

具体问题：

- [major] Invents subgroup numerator/denominator, denies supplied12-month outcome and calls a planned endpoint published outcome.；支持证据ID：E1, E2。
- [moderate] Imposes unsupported tissue-eradication criterion and omits overall16% eradication, making the practical question seem less answerable than supplied culture evidence permits.；支持证据ID：E1, E4。

## epoch2

Core evidence distinction is useful, but overrestrictive lung-tissue interpretation and fabricated specimen language reduce fidelity.

优点：

- Identifies retrospective clearance signal and correctly treats STAR-ter as protocol rather than completed efficacy trial.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Invents microscopy limitation and does not explain historical external placebo comparator. Overstates inability to interpret specific regimen clearance while omitting12-month low overall rate and untreated clearances.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E3。
