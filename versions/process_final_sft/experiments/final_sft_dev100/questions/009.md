## base

The cautious overall conclusion does not repair fabrication of a negative clinical trial result.

优点：

- Recognizes that vascular clinical benefit remains inconclusive and notes the actual metabolic correlations, small sample and heterogeneous surgical context.

具体问题：

- [major] These are ongoing-trial protocol passages specifying planned endpoints, not reported null results. Converts a plan into an actual finding.；支持证据ID：E1, E3。
- [moderate] Underuses the explicit same-cohort marker-leakage reduction narrative and does not clearly distinguish a null metabolome finding from clinical organ-injury outcomes.；支持证据ID：E4, E7。
