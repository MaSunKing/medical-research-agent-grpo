## epoch2

- [major] Group2 specifically received weekly paclitaxel with three-weekly carboplatin; false regimen undermines claimed noncomparability.；支持证据ID：E2。
- [moderate] No significant survival difference does not establish equivalence; weekly treatment had poorer quality of life during treatment.；支持证据ID：E3, E8, E12。
