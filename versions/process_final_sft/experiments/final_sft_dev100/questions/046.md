## epoch1

Useful headline undermined by fabricated design details and overstatement.

优点：

- Correct main ICON8 conclusion and weekly/three-weekly arm structure.

具体问题：

- [major] Sample size is unsupported and cycles were 21 days, not two-weekly; radiation analysis is invented.；支持证据ID：E2。
- [moderate] Turns possible explanation into proven subgroup effect and absence of weekly superiority into evidence of survival superiority for three-weekly.；支持证据ID：E3。

## epoch1_5

Correct directional conclusion but clinically important fabricated dosing and eligibility.

优点：

- Reports European population survival result and nine-month QoL distinction.

具体问题：

- [major] Eligibility was 18 or older without this upper limit; carboplatin was AUC2 weekly, not80mg/m2. Omits the weekly-paclitaxel/three-weekly-carboplatin arm.；支持证据ID：E2。
- [moderate] Unsupported characterization of supplied randomized phaseIII trial.；支持证据ID：E13。

## epoch2

Main survival result and population contrast useful, but regimen inversion is substantial.

优点：

- Correctly distinguishes predominantly European ICON8 from positive Japanese trial and notes missing numerical OS estimates.

具体问题：

- [major] Group2 specifically received weekly paclitaxel with three-weekly carboplatin; false regimen undermines claimed noncomparability.；支持证据ID：E2。
- [moderate] No significant survival difference does not establish equivalence; weekly treatment had poorer quality of life during treatment.；支持证据ID：E3, E8, E12。
