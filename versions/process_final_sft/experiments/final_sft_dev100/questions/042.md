## base

Clear and faithful main contrast with incomplete tradeoff integration.

优点：

- Accurately reports randomized live-birth effect and cohort cycle-level contrast with good-prognosis qualification.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Omits higher failure-to-transfer risk and more frozen embryos with earlier transfer,important to judging overall success. Does not clearly explain fresh/per-couple versus cumulative per-cycle denominators and retrospective cohort limits.；支持证据ID：E1, E3。

## epoch1

Unreliable and largely incoherent synthesis despite some correct figures.

优点：

- Mentions selected good-prognosis benefit and cohort cycle-level rates.

具体问题：

- [major] Source reports odds ratio, does not supply those couple totals, and freezing estimate comes from 9 trials.；支持证据ID：E1, E2。
- [major] Extensive incoherent multilingual text invents clinical outcomes and prevents interpretation of cohort evidence.；支持证据ID：E3, E4。

## epoch1_5

Relevant statistical contrast but misses major tradeoffs and adds unsupported limitations.

优点：

- Correctly reports OR and cohort adjusted OR with distinct denominators.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Overstates the timing-window limitation and omits cancellation and freezing tradeoffs needed for the question.；支持证据ID：E1。
- [moderate] Cohort reports per embryo transfer, not per embryo; infection-protocol qualification is unsupported.；支持证据ID：E3, E6。

## epoch2

Good breadth undermined by a major endpoint-number substitution.

优点：

- Reports live-birth OR and cycle-level cohort contrast and follow-up limitation.

具体问题：

- [major] 29.4% is a live-birth rate; cancellation is 2.8% versus 8.9%.；支持证据ID：E1。
- [moderate] Garbled wording and over-restrictive interpretation obscure directly relevant cleavage versus blastocyst evidence.；支持证据ID：E1, E2。
