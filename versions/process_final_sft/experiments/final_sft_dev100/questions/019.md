## base

Strong source-backed accuracy synthesis; antibody-target wording slightly overgeneralizes otherwise correct figures.

优点：

- Integrates weekly sensitivity, assay differences, commercial-kit gap, specificity controls and major bias/applicability limitations with accurate numbers.

具体问题：

- [minor] These method figures specifically concern combinedIgM-or-IgG, not any single antibody class;weekly figures also pool the combined target and should be labeled explicitly.；支持证据ID：E2, E14。

## epoch1

Relevant accuracy domains covered but several method-specific labels and estimates are invented.

优点：

- Provides specificity figures, rising weekly sensitivity and selection/vaccination limits.

具体问题：

- [major] Weekly figures pool IgG-or-IgM across methods; extra method ranges are unsupported, and IgG can equal/exceed combined sensitivity in E14.；支持证据ID：E2, E14, E15。

## epoch1_5

Mostly accurate table but source/population conflation and missing timing reduce explanatory value.

优点：

- Most method/antibody table values accurately reproduce the later review.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Conflates separate reviews; IgM LFIA CI is64–67%, while49–78% belongs to commercial-kit subgroup. Commercial specificity difference is not supplied.；支持证据ID：E2, E12, E13, E14。
- [moderate] Misses suspect-group lower specificity and the major symptom-week sensitivity gradient.；支持证据ID：E2, E6。

## epoch2

Sensitivity-specificity conflation and omission of timing dominate otherwise appropriate bias cautions.

优点：

- Names reference standards and gives the older ELISA sensitivity correctly.

具体问题：

- [major] These are specificity values placed under sensitivity in malformed rows, materially misleading test interpretation.；支持证据ID：E14, E15。
- [moderate] LFIA IgG is73%; omits crucial41.1%,74.9%,88.0% week-dependent sensitivity despite abundant supplied evidence.；支持证据ID：E2, E14。
