## base

Strong central data extraction but unsupported certainty contrast and omitted safety boundaries weaken calibration.

优点：

- Accurately reports pooled sitting-lateral RR, prone-lateral direction and NeoCLEAR success/desaturation results.

具体问题：

- [moderate] No supplied explicit high-quality rating for NeoCLEAR; sitting-versus-lying overlaps the lateral comparison called low certainty. Needs term-newborn applicability, missing prone harms and unreliable pain/time outcomes to avoid overconfident universal preference.；支持证据ID：E3, E4, E5, E8。
- [minor] Numerical RR is lateral relative to prone, not prone relative to lateral, though prose gives correct direction.；支持证据ID：E3。

## epoch1

Contains useful core data but fabricates procedural details and weakens supported safety conclusions.

优点：

- Reports NeoCLEAR success rates and oxygen advantage, alongside review's little/no sitting-lateral success difference.

具体问题：

- [major] Invents timing/procedural details and a prone-sitting comparison; enrollment includes preterms though mostly term. Garbled posterior-period wording obscures interpretation.；支持证据ID：E3, E5, E6, E8。
- [moderate] Understates the explicit likely lower bradycardia/desaturation with sitting; older-child surgical anesthesia adds no positioning benefit evidence for infants.；支持证据ID：E3, E8。

## epoch1_5

Extensive hallucinated harms, source design/population errors and incoherence prevent trustworthy comparison.

优点：

- Reports NeoCLEAR success percentages and the prone-lateral direction.

具体问题：

- [major] Invents harms and sitting-prone benefit; misclassifies randomized review and attributes older-child noncomparative anesthesia findings to infants. Bradycardia/desaturation comparison is sitting-lateral, not prone-lateral 171 infants.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E3, E5, E9。

## epoch2

Accurate comparative table is surrounded by fabricated anatomy/harms and irrelevant fragments.

优点：

- Table accurately lists main RR/CI and success percentages; states no direct sitting-prone comparison.

具体问题：

- [major] Unsupported severe neurologic-risk hierarchy and invented lateral-position taxonomy/table; E1 is older children undergoing anesthesia, not a positional infant safety comparison.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E5, E9。
- [moderate] Trial's reported successes imply roughly 36–42% failures, not nearly half; age/generalizability limits are poorly integrated.；支持证据ID：E5。
