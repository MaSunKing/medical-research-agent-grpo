## base

Very limited evidence integration and false missing-outcome claim prevent an adequate effectiveness assessment.

优点：

- Recognizes possible knowledge benefit and lack of proven ability to secure emergency care.

具体问题：

- [major] E2 provides symptom-to-treatment timing, three null/one shorter-delay studies and moderate-certainty little/no mortality effect, all omitted. E1 is background/methods and cannot support the cited findings; anxiety certainty is moderate, not a blanket low rating.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E3。

## epoch1

Useful scope and outcome summary but overstates anxiety/knowledge benefit and underuses timing results.

优点：

- Correctly defines after-contact escalation and highlights uncertain timing and little mortality effect.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Few adverse anxiety effects is not demonstrated anxiety reduction; knowledge may improve, not probably; three no-difference/one-shorter comparison results are supplied.；支持证据ID：E2, E3。
