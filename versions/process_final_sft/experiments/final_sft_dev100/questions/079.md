## base

Useful specific estimate, but categorical opening and omitted pooled evidence weaken uncertainty calibration.

优点：

- Correct atosiban RR/CI, sample size and comparator, plus acknowledged bias/imprecision and limited other-drug evidence.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Uncertain/non-significant findings do not prove no improvement; E4 supplies pooled placebo/nil live-birth RR1.09 CI0.98–1.20, which is omitted. Clinical pregnancy versus live birth distinction is also unused.；支持证据ID：E1, E3, E4。

## epoch1

Right clinical endpoint and overall caution, but materially confuses study counts and comparators.

优点：

- Correctly identifies no established live-birth benefit and reports atosiban RR/absolute range.

具体问题：

- [major] 3611 is all 11 trials' clinical pregnancy analysis, not nolasiban live birth; studies include three drugs/routes. RR is relative rather than absolute risk ratio.；支持证据ID：E1, E3, E4。

## epoch1_5

Generally accurate evidence use and answer, with some imprecise certainty and unnecessary garbled wording.

优点：

- Correctly distinguishes pooled live-birth sample, atosiban 800-person result and limited barusiban evidence.

具体问题：

- [minor] No established live-birth improvement is not proof of unchanged outcomes; search date March 2021 is supplied. 'Enjoyment dates and placemaking' is meaningless.；支持证据ID：E2, E3。

## epoch2

Core question answered with calibrated uncertainty; one important denominator conflation.

优点：

- Separates live birth from clinical pregnancy and reports overall live-birth RR accurately.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Nine studies/3733 are all drugs combined; atosiban live-birth estimate uses one RCT/800 women. Chemotherapy/implant-route caveats are irrelevant.；支持证据ID：E1。
