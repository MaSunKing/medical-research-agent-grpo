## base

Generally useful supported summary, with modest overcausal disease wording and incomplete concrete causes.

优点：

- Answers both causes and health relationship, including structural factors and common conditions, without inventing quantitative risks.

具体问题：

- [minor] Specific causal development of listed diseases is not established by their being common; omits supplied intimate-partner violence, bereavement/divorce, justice involvement, disabilities and child trauma consequences.；支持证据ID：E5, E6。

## epoch1

Useful general explanation weakened by fabricated terminology and unjustified denial of explicit trauma consequences.

优点：

- Describes feedback cycle and several relevant causes and health conditions.

具体问题：

- [moderate] E5 explicitly describes emotional/behavioral risk in children following abuse/assault; ampullary infections are not listed. Omits unemployment, household losses and malnutrition.；支持证据ID：E5, E6。

## epoch1_5

Answers both questions reasonably but incomplete disease/trauma coverage and mild population embellishment limit evidence use.

优点：

具体问题：
