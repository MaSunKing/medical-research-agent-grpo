优点：

- Answers both causes and health relationship, including structural factors and common conditions, without inventing quantitative risks.

具体问题：

- [minor] Specific causal development of listed diseases is not established by their being common; omits supplied intimate-partner violence, bereavement/divorce, justice involvement, disabilities and child trauma consequences.；支持证据ID：E5, E6。
