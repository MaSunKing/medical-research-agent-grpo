## base

Correct principal drug/time findings coexist with an unsupported agitation benefit and false comparator/evidence-gap statements.

优点：

- Correctly identifies risperidone agitation benefit and pimavanserin6-week psychosis benefit with no significant12-week advantage.

具体问题：

- [major] Only one RCT is represented, secondary agitation/aggression outcomes show no significant difference, and reported agitation adverse events are more frequent with pimavanserin. Claim of reduction is unsupported.；支持证据ID：E5, E9, E12。
- [moderate] Trials compare placebo, not no treatment; E12 supplies placebo psychosis changes/response. Risperidone includes vascular dementia, although subtype-specific results are absent. Broad class/population opening overextends evidence.；支持证据ID：E1, E11, E12。

## epoch1

Useful efficacy distinction, but unsupported treatment ranking and poor safety integration limit reliability.

优点：

- Distinguishes agitation from psychosis, correctly reports pimavanserin short-lived advantage and limits dementia-subtype inference.

具体问题：

- [moderate] No head-to-head evidence supports drug preference, adherence discrepancy is unsupported, and psychosis should remain a distinct outcome. Omits important available somnolence/agitation/edema safety context.；支持证据ID：E1, E5, E12。

## epoch1_5

Mostly appropriate high-level answer but several endpoint/population misreadings obscure supported effects and harms.

优点：

- Correctly separates risperidone benefits from pimavanserin short-term psychosis signal and limited vascular-specific inference.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Extrapyramidal symptoms are not loss of involuntary movement; E1 explicitly reports nonaggression subscale and agitation benefit, and pimavanserin adverse-event proportions refer overall trial rather than established severe subgroup. Garbled subgroup passage reduces clarity.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E5, E12。

## epoch2

Generally strong relevant synthesis with a few overstatements of temporal certainty and null-result meaning.

优点：

- Accurately reports risperidone12-week benefit, pimavanserin6-week MD/CI, absence of durable advantage and no active comparison.

具体问题：

- [minor] 12-week endpoint does not establish sustained unequivocal reduction; no significant between-group advantage at12weeks is not no numerical symptom improvement. E1 is one trial, not a pooled study.；支持证据ID：E1, E12。
