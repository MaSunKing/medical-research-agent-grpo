## base

Generally faithful principal effects, with an unnecessary comparator detour and incomplete calibration of general surgical applicability.

优点：

- Correctly reports the principal CGA risk ratios, certainty and hospital-stay/cost benefits, without reversing discharge direction.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Follows the Checklist's altered comparator rather than the original question; the actual comparison is CGA versus usual surgical care. The key applicability limitation is evidence mostly from hip-fracture patients, not missing nonspecialist comparisons.；支持证据ID：E2, E3, E8。
- [minor] Omits the available no-clear-readmission-change and uncertain complications results, and does not explicitly discuss the mortality interval crossing1.；支持证据ID：E1, E12。

## epoch1

Less distorted than the alternatives, but leaves substantial directly useful evidence unused and remains distracted by the checklist's incorrect comparator.

优点：

- Keeps the conditional benefit for hip-fracture patients and avoids extending it confidently to all surgeries.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Omits the available probably shorter hospital stay and little/no readmission difference; does not explain the strong discharge finding with its actual reduced-increased-care endpoint.；支持证据ID：E1, E12。
- [moderate] Uses much of a short answer to pursue a mistaken specialist-comparator interpretation instead of explaining the CGA intervention and recovery outcomes.；支持证据ID：E2, E3, E7。

## epoch1_5

Broad numerical coverage is not sufficient when the discharge benefit is explicitly reversed and unsupported qualifications are added.

优点：

- Attempts an outcome-by-outcome synthesis and reports mortality, readmission and cost data from E1.

具体问题：

- [major] Reverses the clinically central result: 0.71 concerns discharge to increased care, not return home. E12 explicitly states patients are more likely to return to the prior location.；支持证据ID：E1, E12。
- [moderate] Overstates the review's probably slight reduction in stay and invents a screening-period carry-over concern absent from the source.；支持证据ID：E1。
- [moderate] Introduces an unsupported specialist-versus-nonspecialist framing rather than consistently answering whether CGA improves recovery.；支持证据ID：E2, E7。

## epoch2

Contains useful source facts but mixes them with nonexistent repair/pooling explanations and a confused main recovery outcome.

优点：

- Reports the mortality RR and CI accurately and identifies the predominance of hip-fracture evidence.

具体问题：

- [major] Invents an evidence repair and a pooling qualification not present in the opened passages. E1 expressly attributes the mortality estimate to hip-fracture patients.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E3。
- [major] Confuses the direction and certainty of discharge benefit. E1 reports reduced discharge to increased care, RR 0.71 with high certainty; E12 says return to the prior residence is more likely.；支持证据ID：E1, E12。
- [moderate] Lets the mistaken checklist comparator displace the user's actual question about CGA and postoperative recovery.；支持证据ID：E2, E7。
