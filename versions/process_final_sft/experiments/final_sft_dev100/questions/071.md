## base

Relevant but substitutes nonexistent population gaps for actual missing direct outcomes and uncertain comparative ranking.

优点：

- Identifies studied aclidinium/formoterol comparators and no significant major-outcome differences among dual bronchodilators.

具体问题：

- [major] Both reviews explicitly restrict stable COPD; E3 supplies moderate-to-severe severity and FEV1. Invented population limitations displace real uncertainty.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E3, E4。
- [moderate] Direct placebo/monotherapy efficacy results are absent, network no-difference does not establish equivalence or safety, and network-assumption sensitivity is omitted.；支持证据ID：E1, E4, E6。

## epoch1

Uses population and network facts but overstates direct efficacy and safety beyond the frozen passages.

优点：

- Accurately summarizes no significant differences among combinations for several major outcomes.

具体问题：

- [major] These opened excerpts do not contain efficacy or comparative placebo safety results. E3 describes postbronchodilator, not prebronchodilator FEV1.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E3。
- [moderate] Invented adverse-event definition absent from supplied text.；支持证据ID：E1。

## epoch1_5

Useful network synthesis is mixed with unsupported direct efficacy and internally inconsistent uncertainty.

优点：

- Recognizes network-dependent rankings and no demonstrated difference in major harms between combinations.

具体问题：

- [major] Opened direct-review excerpts describe methods and participants, not efficacy results; the answer later contradicts its own placebo-benefit claim.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E3。
- [moderate] Opened results do not establish this separate endpoint; the reported no-difference result concerns all-cause mortality.；支持证据ID：E4, E5。

## epoch2

Central treatment attribution and safety statements are unreliable despite some correct network limitations.

优点：

- Recognizes that absence of significant differences does not prove equal safety.

具体问题：

- [major] E11 attributes this head-to-head benefit to umeclidinium/vilanterol, not aclidinium/formoterol. Direct placebo/monotherapy symptom and exacerbation benefits also are not supplied.；支持证据ID：E1, E3, E11。
- [major] No-difference results do not mean fewer events, and the network passages are full text; cardiovascular mortality no-difference is not supplied.；支持证据ID：E4, E5, E6。
