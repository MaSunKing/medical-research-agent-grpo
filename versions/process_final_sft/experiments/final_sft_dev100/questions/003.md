## base

Mostly useful and concise evidence handling; its opening needs to distinguish no direct outcome evidence from very uncertain evidence of benefit.

优点：

- Keeps bacterial contamination distinct from infection transmission and correctly states no direct disease-transmission outcomes or viral/fungal studies were measured.

具体问题：

- [moderate] The opening implies evidence of prevention when none of these studies measured transmission. The later sentences correctly clarify that this clinical outcome is unresolved.；支持证据ID：E5。
- [minor] Does not include the separately opened mouth-rinse review's consistent direct-outcome gap, although the device review alone supports the main uncertain conclusion.；支持证据ID：E3。

## epoch1

Correct high-level distinction is undermined by cross-review mixing and invented material.

优点：

- Recognizes that aerosol bacterial measurements do not establish protection from infection.

具体问题：

- [major] Moves the 16-study count from the device review to the rinse review and then changes studies to methods; the rinse passages supply no such count.；支持证据ID：E3, E5。
- [major] Adds incoherent measurement domains absent from all opened evidence.；支持证据ID：E5。
- [moderate] The categorical opening and concluding suggestion of established microbial improvement overstate what the very-low-certainty device review can establish.；支持证据ID：E5。

## epoch1_5

Substantially uses the relevant evidence to answer the actual transmission question without inventing a preventive effect.

优点：

- Separates mouth-rinse and aerosol-device reviews and consistently distinguishes bacterial contamination from actual infection transmission; retains low/very-low certainty and review date.

具体问题：

- [minor] The headline is less precise than the body: the key finding is no direct transmission-outcome measurements, not evidence of no prevention effect. It could also state more explicitly that the device evidence cannot establish even a bacterial-contamination effect.；支持证据ID：E3, E5。

## epoch2

Mostly relevant and concise, but its concluding surrogate-benefit claim is more certain than the supplied reviews.

优点：

- Directly addresses transmission and names relevant approaches; identifies low certainty and absent virus/fungus outcomes.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Generalizes beyond the reviewed findings: E5 explicitly says the evidence is too uncertain to deduce an effect on bacterial contamination. E3 gives only low-certainty superiority of CHX over selected rinses, not a blanket effect of all measures.；支持证据ID：E3, E5。
