## base

Mostly faithful with a valuable scope distinction, though some positive framing is stronger than the available indirect comparisons.

优点：

- Correct nursing RR/CI and explicitly acknowledges broader professional-support evidence cannot isolate individual counselling alone.

具体问题：

- [minor] Cardiovascular subgroup comparison is limited indirect evidence, not firm superiority. Opening somewhat exceeds the later appropriate individual-only caveat; combination-treatment findings and six-month horizon are omitted.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E5。

## epoch1

Conflates intervention formats and analysis populations, despite an appropriate final limitation.

优点：

- Reports both pooled RRs and eventually acknowledges individual-session isolation limits.

具体问题：

- [major] 30–300 minutes is maximum total contact; RR1.82 uses 40 studies/15021 after excluding the large outlier. Neither combined pharmacotherapy nor broad nursing interventions isolate individual counselling.；支持证据ID：E4, E5。

## epoch1_5

Central numeric and comparator misinterpretations undermine the answer.

优点：

- Notes nursing benefit and that brief non-specialist interventions have weaker evidence.

具体问题：

- [major] RR1.82 is 82% relative increase, not 18%; subgroup RRs are separate comparisons to controls, not a direct specialist-versus-usual-provider range.；支持证据ID：E5。
- [major] Combination-treatment benefit cannot isolate individual counselling alone.；支持证据ID：E3, E5。

## epoch2

Accurate nursing result and a useful later caveat, but overclaims direct individual-counselling evidence and muddles the combination review.

优点：

- Correctly reports nursing RR 1.29 as a 29% relative increase, not an absolute increase.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Nursing evidence is not established as exclusively individual; controls are described, and combination-treatment benefit should not be confused with lack of indirect intensity effect.；支持证据ID：E1, E3, E5, E7。
