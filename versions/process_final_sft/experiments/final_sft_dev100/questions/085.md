## base

Broad quantitative coverage but treats uncertainty too much as null effect and fails to synthesize a key conflicting survival result.

优点：

- Reports mortality, transplantation, SAE and multi-organ estimates correctly; acknowledges overall uncertainty and unclear individual harms.

具体问题：

- [moderate] RR0.40 with wide CI means very uncertain effect, not established little/no effect. Omits separate NBALSS low-certainty OS benefit RR1.42 while using that review's biochemical results; bilirubin/ALT certainty is moderate/low, not all very low. Missing QoL is unmentioned.；支持证据ID：E2, E4, E5, E8。

## epoch1

Covers many outcomes but misstates system counts and suppresses important survival evidence from the second review.

优点：

- Identifies very uncertain mortality/safety evidence and absent quality-of-life data.

具体问题：

- [major] Actual split is eight artificial/three bioartificial; no five missing trials. Rate ratios are mislabeled risk ratios and imprecise estimates do not establish near-identical safety.；支持证据ID：E2, E4, E6, E8。
- [moderate] Fails to report the separate review's significant low-certainty OS benefit, obscuring the key cross-review conflict; adds units not supplied.；支持证据ID：E4, E5。

## epoch1_5

Substantial quantitative coverage, but direction/metric errors and dismissive handling of conflicting review reduce reliability.

优点：

- Reports principal mortality, transplant and organ-failure estimates and absence of a clear death benefit in the broad review.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Transplant RR does not establish benefit; separate randomized meta-analysis is not merely descriptive and reports reductions in TNF/IL6 with very low certainty. Age10–69 describes all participants, including a few minors, not children as a group.；支持证据ID：E4, E5, E6。
- [moderate] 0.93 rate ratio is a relative estimate, not 7 percentage-point difference or comparison of separate group CIs.；支持证据ID：E2, E6。

## epoch2

Strong coverage and calibrated comparison with limited unsupported methodological wording.

优点：

- Explicitly contrasts low-certainty NBALSS OS benefit with very uncertain broader mortality findings; covers transplant, encephalopathy, harms and missing QoL.

具体问题：

- [minor] Logistic analysis/transport durations are not supplied and broad fewer-complication language is stronger than the very uncertain results.；支持证据ID：E4, E6。
