## base

Better numerical fidelity and treatment caveats than many alternatives, but still insufficiently separates pooled mixed-cancer effects from the gastric-cancer question.

优点：

- Reports the pooled survival and severe-adverse-event figures correctly, answers both benefits and harms, and explicitly recognizes that multimodal therapy does not isolate intraperitoneal chemotherapy.

具体问题：

- [moderate] These are mixed-cancer and mixed-regimen pooled estimates, not stomach-specific estimates; controls also include control-based regimens. The treatment-modality caveat does not fully resolve this population/comparator transfer.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E9, E13。

## epoch1

Best integrates the relevant pooled benefit/harm trade-off here, but still misbinds sample size and one follow-up result; not a fully reliable answer.

优点：

- Uses pooled survival, three-year mortality and adverse events, while repeatedly warning that multiple cancers and additional therapies prevent a gastric-HIPEC-only estimate.

具体问题：

- [major] 3,925belongs to the overall13-study review, not the seven-study OS subset. E13 reports five-year RR0.88(CI0.79–0.99), so declaring the pooled five-year effect unclear misstates the supplied result.；支持证据ID：E1, E13。
- [moderate] The review explicitly includes varied multimodal regimens; the source's standardized inclusion reporting does not justify declaring these findings must be omitted.；支持证据ID：E2, E5。

## epoch1_5

Reasonable narrative limits do not excuse unsupported gastric sample size and incorrect harm/heterogeneity interpretation.

优点：

- Clearly separates pooled multimodal findings from a stomach-cancer-specific effect and identifies digestive/circulatory toxicity.

具体问题：

- [major] The opened table lists study settings but supplies no200-patient denominator; this number is fabricated.；支持证据ID：E13, E14。
- [major] The source reports an unlabelled-as-adjusted risk ratio, not an adjusted hazard ratio. This changes the harm effect measure.；支持证据ID：E3。
- [moderate] E13 explicitly describes low heterogeneity (P0.26), not significant heterogeneity.；支持证据ID：E13。

## epoch2

The relevant caution is undermined by invented population size and gastric-specific efficacy, exactly the scope distinction the question requires.

优点：

- Addresses both survival and severe adverse events and explicitly notes that mixed cancer/treatment evidence cannot isolate gastric intraperitoneal chemotherapy.

具体问题：

- [major] 4,031 is unsupported (overall review has13studies/3,925patients), and the pooled seven-study HR0.86 is wrongly assigned to the gastric subgroup.；支持证据ID：E1, E13。
- [moderate] E9 says9of13, not about half; this misdescribes the treatment mixture underlying the caution.；支持证据ID：E9。
