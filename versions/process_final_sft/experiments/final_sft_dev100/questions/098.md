## base

Mostly faithful endpoint descriptions and useful caveat, but overgeneralizes them to lid inflammation before narrowing scope.

优点：

- Accurately describes DED symptom/sign outcomes for established drugs and MGD gland-function effects; notes missing lid-specific effect estimates and durability.

具体问题：

- [moderate] DED corneal/conjunctival and MGD gland-function endpoints do not directly establish anti-inflammatory eyelid efficacy; final caveat should control the opening conclusion. Timeframes and available quantitative symptom effects are underused.；支持证据ID：E1, E4, E5, E10。

## epoch1

Scope distinction is valid but trial outcomes, units and direction are repeatedly mishandled.

优点：

- Recognizes that DED outcomes alone cannot establish general eyelid-inflammation efficacy.

具体问题：

- [major] Invents lifitegrast lid assessments;6.1/10.5 are within-arm changes, not vehicle-adjusted differences; CFS is corneal, comparator hypotonic saline, and E10 explicitly shows significant improvement, contradicting final denial.；支持证据ID：E1, E5, E7, E10。

## epoch1_5

Good source-scope caution but quantitative embellishment and omission of explicit blepharitis signs weaken usefulness.

优点：

- Appropriately distinguishes DED trial outcomes from lid-specific anti-inflammatory efficacy and describes several symptom/sign signals.

具体问题：

- [moderate] No clinical-importance threshold is supplied;3-month follow-up is explicit; invents licaminlimab0.5–0.6-point lid result (SANDE−7.9 versus−3.6). Omits directly lid-related lotilaner erythema result.；支持证据ID：E5, E7, E12。

## epoch2

Unsupported numbers and contradictory categorical dismissal of explicit lid findings undermine both faithfulness and calibration.

优点：

- Mentions actual AZR3-month results and lotilaner6-week Demodex/erythema findings.

具体问题：

- [major] Invents scale expansion and extra scores. Lotilaner table describes mite eradication/erythema cure, not general symptom cure; denying lid-sign evidence directly contradicts its own reported eyelid erythema finding.；支持证据ID：E5, E7。
