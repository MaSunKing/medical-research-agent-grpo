## base

Unsupported confident ranking relies on a different orthodontic condition and ignores explicit uncertainty.

优点：

- Identifies fixed appliances as one studied treatment category.

具体问题：

- [major] Very-low-certainty small single studies do not establish a best treatment;general braces superiority is unsupported and alternatives/extraction tradeoffs are omitted.；支持证据ID：E2, E3。
- [major] These passages concern prominent upper front teeth and early-versus-adolescent timing,not fixed-versus-functional crowding superiority.；支持证据ID：E10, E12。

## epoch1

Direct and appropriately cautious answer with only minor terminology/time qualification errors.

优点：

- Centers inability to identify a best treatment and integrates lip bumper, Schwarz and both extraction findings with uncertainty and arch-length tradeoff.

具体问题：

- [minor] Opened lip-bumper result does not specify one year and reports a confidence interval.；支持证据ID：E2。

## epoch1_5

Conflates distinct orthodontic conditions and loses the directly relevant numerical evidence.

优点：

- Recognizes low certainty and some indirectness of Class II evidence.

具体问题：

- [major] Unsupported ranking and imports the prominent-upper-teeth review sample into the crowding review, which has 24 trials and 1512 participants.；支持证据ID：E2, E3, E10。
- [moderate] Uses crossbite population as overall scope and omits coaxial qualifier in the wire comparison.；支持证据ID：E7, E11。

## epoch2

Some useful direct evidence but unsupported treatment ranking controls the answer.

优点：

- Reports lip-bumper and deciduous extraction effects and recognizes crossbite evidence is indirect.

具体问题：

- [major] Study counts and unrelated overjet evidence do not rank fixed braces as best; review is very low certainty.；支持证据ID：E2, E3, E10。
- [moderate] Fails to convey the extraction tradeoff of reduced arch length.；支持证据ID：E3。
