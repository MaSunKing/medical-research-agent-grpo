## base

Generally sound brief answer but conflates descriptive cure populations and leaves the principal comparative evidence underused.

优点：

- Recognizes potential topical efficacy, low certainty, formulation gaps and reported antifungal comparisons.

具体问题：

- [moderate] 74.9% is all treatment arms, not clotrimazole specifically; placebo benefit is unreported rather than an equivalence question. Omits direct povidone-iodine RR and biased-study sensitivity while emphasizing formulation uncertainty.；支持证据ID：E4, E9, E13。

## epoch2

Covers key limitations but misstates study eligibility and the populations behind pooled statistics.

优点：

- Explains missing cream-versus-drop and placebo estimates, regional scope and imprecision.

具体问题：

- [major] Review explicitly includes nonrandomized comparisons; RR 0.86 pools four studies including the biased nonrandomized one, not randomized studies alone.；支持证据ID：E3, E4, E9, E13。
- [moderate] 74.9% is across all agents, including antiseptics; descriptive response does not establish an azole-specific causal effect.；支持证据ID：E4。
