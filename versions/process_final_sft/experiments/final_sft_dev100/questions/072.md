## base

Generally sound brief answer but conflates descriptive cure populations and leaves the principal comparative evidence underused.

优点：

- Recognizes potential topical efficacy, low certainty, formulation gaps and reported antifungal comparisons.

具体问题：

- [moderate] 74.9% is all treatment arms, not clotrimazole specifically; placebo benefit is unreported rather than an equivalence question. Omits direct povidone-iodine RR and biased-study sensitivity while emphasizing formulation uncertainty.；支持证据ID：E4, E9, E13。

## epoch1

Several relevant facts are covered, but a central comparative statistic is misinterpreted and prose is degraded.

优点：

- Separates descriptive cure rates from direct comparisons and identifies placebo/formulation gaps.

具体问题：

- [major] Converts RR 0.95 to absolute 95% cure; this is not an absolute response rate.；支持证据ID：E9, E13。
- [moderate] Invents an eligibility cutoff. Mixed-language and nonsensical fragments also obscure interpretation.；支持证据ID：E5。

## epoch1_5

Generally useful comparison with accurate numbers, undermined by invented methodological and safety qualifications.

优点：

- Correctly reports clotrimazole descriptive rates, primary RR and randomized-only sensitivity estimate without claiming equivalence.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Invents design proportions and a separate review/assessment limitation absent from the supplied review.；支持证据ID：E3, E7, E13。
- [moderate] Confuses the source's restricted-azole-access advice for antiseptics with clotrimazole, which E2 calls non-ototoxic; garbled phrases reduce clarity.；支持证据ID：E2。

## epoch2

Covers key limitations but misstates study eligibility and the populations behind pooled statistics.

优点：

- Explains missing cream-versus-drop and placebo estimates, regional scope and imprecision.

具体问题：

- [major] Review explicitly includes nonrandomized comparisons; RR 0.86 pools four studies including the biased nonrandomized one, not randomized studies alone.；支持证据ID：E3, E4, E9, E13。
- [moderate] 74.9% is across all agents, including antiseptics; descriptive response does not establish an azole-specific causal effect.；支持证据ID：E4。
