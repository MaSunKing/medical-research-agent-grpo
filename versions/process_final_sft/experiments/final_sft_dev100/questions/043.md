优点：

具体问题：

- [moderate] Controls are usual/no tobacco education and trials randomized;unsupported control ambiguity unnecessarily weakens the demonstrated educational contrast.；支持证据ID：E2, E3。

## epoch1

Correct general answer but invents method-specific patient effects.

优点：

- Identifies students and limits longer-term inference.

具体问题：

- [major] Assigns the pooled cessation effect separately to each training method; source method-specific effects concern counselling skills.；支持证据ID：E1, E2。
- [moderate] Changes cessation to attempts and discounts the explicitly tobacco-specific educational comparison.；支持证据ID：E2。

## epoch1_5

Useful cessation figures mixed with invented study details and restrictions.

优点：

- Reports the three-study sample and absolute cessation benefit.

具体问题：

- [major] OR is 2.02, and individual comparator breakdown is not supplied.；支持证据ID：E1, E2。
- [moderate] 4343 counts students; 3122 patients separately. Excluding established smokers is unsupported, and skill heterogeneity is misapplied to quitting.；支持证据ID：E1, E2。
