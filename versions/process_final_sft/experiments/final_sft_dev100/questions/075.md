## base

Captures weekly tradeoff but confuses the key daily-insulin benefit endpoint and ends with an overly uniform conclusion.

优点：

- Correctly reports weekly insulin's similar HbA1c and increased severe-hypoglycaemia rate, and glargine's nonsignificant comparison.

具体问题：

- [major] CI0.39–1.17 does not establish nocturnal benefit; the supported benefit is overall severe hypoglycaemia RR0.69 CI0.52–0.92, with prediction inconsistency, which is omitted.；支持证据ID：E4, E5, E14。
- [moderate] Overgeneralizes despite explicit weekly severe-hypoglycaemia disadvantage; does not keep adult-only weekly scope clear.；支持证据ID：E7。

## epoch1

Mostly useful drug-specific comparison with some class/population/design overstatement.

优点：

- Reports detemir severe-hypoglycaemia proportions/RR and weekly safety tradeoff; recognizes missing long-term patient outcomes.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Overgeneralizes detemir's finding to the analogue class and adults only; severe-hypoglycaemia estimate uses eight studies, and prediction interval concerns a new study rather than specifically longer follow-up.；支持证据ID：E4, E5, E6。

## epoch1_5

Coverage is broad but statistical misinterpretation and sweeping contradictory caveats impair calibration.

优点：

- Correctly identifies detemir's central benefit, no NPH-degludec trials and increased severe hypoglycaemia with weekly therapy.

具体问题：

- [major] Confuses prediction interval with CI: RR CI 0.52–0.92 excludes an increase; prediction interval 0.34–1.39 spans it.；支持证据ID：E4, E5。
- [moderate] Contradicts the directly T1D-specific efficacy findings and overextends absent/different comparisons.；支持证据ID：E1, E7。

## epoch2

Multiple central outcome/comparator and absolute-benefit errors make the basal-insulin summary unreliable.

优点：

- Includes once-weekly insulin's similar glycaemic efficacy and increased severe hypoglycaemia.

具体问题：

- [major] E5 reports 36 fewer, with baseline 115/1000; detemir benefit is severe overall, not nocturnal. The 2925 participants belong to detemir-versus-NPH nocturnal outcome, not detemir-versus-glargine.；支持证据ID：E3, E4, E5, E14。
