## base

Useful relevant synthesis, mildly overstates subgroup certainty and leaves some available intervention coverage unused.

优点：

- Distinguishes retrospective PAE comparison from conservative-treatment descriptions and accurately states fever/infection/pain directions.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Higher stage predicts failure; E2 suggests usefulness but subgroup-specific superiority is not demonstrated. Durability is suggested by lower recurrence, with assessment timing unknown.；支持证据ID：E2, E3。
- [minor] Omits selected palliative radiotherapy/topical agents and available control/recurrence magnitudes; comparative certainty should explicitly distinguish association from causation.；支持证据ID：E1, E3。

## epoch1

Some central data are accurate, but irrelevant fragments, a population error and omissions impair the synthesis.

优点：

- Reports hemostatic and recurrence rates correctly and notes missing timing and randomized evidence.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Stage ≥IIIA is a risk-factor result, not stated cohort eligibility; fertilization and manufacture-specific language are irrelevant invented framing.；支持证据ID：E1, E3。
- [moderate] Omits actual fever/infection/pain tradeoffs and selected radiotherapy while offering vague safety benefit language.；支持证据ID：E1, E3。

## epoch1_5

Answers the principal comparisons but blurs source design and observed versus theoretical effects.

优点：

- Accurately reports group sizes and bleeding-control/recurrence rates and recognizes retrospective limits.

具体问题：

- [moderate] E1 is narrative; faster control is theoretical in E3, not an observed outcome; packing success is explicitly 57.8%.；支持证据ID：E1, E3。
- [minor] Omits selected palliative radiotherapy, a directly relevant available option.；支持证据ID：E1。

## epoch2

具体问题：
