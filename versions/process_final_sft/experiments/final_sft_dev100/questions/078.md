## base

Reasonable uncertain conclusion, but denies observed sustained response in a minority and treats a symptom-severity scale as irrelevant to symptoms.

优点：

- Recognizes inconsistent trial summaries and weak overall efficacy evidence; separates CCS follow-up from sham data.

具体问题：

- [moderate] 29% of initial responders retained improvement at24months. CCS measures angina severity; lack of control, not absence of symptom meaning, limits inference. No-control design and adverse-event excess should be explicitly integrated.；支持证据ID：E4, E5。

## epoch1

Appropriate broad caution becomes inaccurate dismissal of reported symptom signals.

优点：

具体问题：

- [major] E8 explicitly calls pain-count reduction significant; 61.5% is of initial responders rather than all 86. CCS change is misdismissed as unrelated to symptom severity.；支持证据ID：E4, E8。
