## base

Broad relevant coverage, but reverses a contextual survey inference and overstates causal intervention support.

优点：

- Covers health beliefs, social norms, trust, access and respectful timely communication; describes feared harms as parental perceptions.

具体问题：

- [major] Study reports parents of nonimmunized children ranked local-leader trust more highly, not that greater trust predicts uptake. It ranks relative decision factors, not causal effects.；支持证据ID：E13, E14。
- [moderate] Qualitative preferences and survey motivations do not demonstrate intervention effectiveness. Regional survey and varying qualitative-confidence limits are not distinguished.；支持证据ID：E10, E11, E12, E14。

## epoch1

Some accurate concepts but poor intelligibility and incomplete concrete factors substantially reduce usefulness.

优点：

- Retains broad themes, respectful/balanced communication and noncausal interpretation of Nigeria survey.

具体问题：

- [major] Broken table and mixed-language fragments make major social/trust explanations difficult to interpret; omits concrete safety/disease-risk beliefs and the survey's good-parenting/social-norm findings despite discussing it.；支持证据ID：E4, E9, E13, E14。

## epoch1_5

Relevant themes are undermined by fabricated details and failure to preserve perception-versus-fact distinction.

优点：

- Names broad health/social/trust/access themes and communication preferences, recognizing qualitative limits.

具体问题：

- [major] Specific beliefs are supplied; invents pathway names, cruelty/lab-tested ineffectiveness and mismatched review-frequency fractions. Concerns are parents' beliefs, not verified vaccine defects.；支持证据ID：E5, E6, E9。

## epoch2

Mostly accurate and relevant, with minor distortion and needless garbled caveats.

优点：

- Clearly groups social, trust, service and individual-belief influences and describes natural-immunity/overload beliefs as beliefs.

具体问题：

- [minor] Control barrier is lack of self-efficacy, not demonstrated improvement. Final mutation language is meaningless and omits useful timely balanced communication and contextual Nigerian ranking.；支持证据ID：E9, E10, E14。
