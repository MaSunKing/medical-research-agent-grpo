## base

优点：

- Accurately summarizes theoretical protection,plausible toxicity/microbiome/local harms,and uncertain clinical efficacy without claiming proven protection.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Does not date no-completed-studies toJune2020or integrate2022recommendation/salivary-load and nasal evidence;thus flattens distinct dates and clinical-versus-surrogate evidence.；支持证据ID：E2, E4, E10, E13。
