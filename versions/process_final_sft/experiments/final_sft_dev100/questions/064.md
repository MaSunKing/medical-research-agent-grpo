## base

Faithful concise Cochrane summary but incomplete use of available later evidence and its limits.

优点：

- Accurately summarizes theoretical protection,plausible toxicity/microbiome/local harms,and uncertain clinical efficacy without claiming proven protection.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Does not date no-completed-studies toJune2020or integrate2022recommendation/salivary-load and nasal evidence;thus flattens distinct dates and clinical-versus-surrogate evidence.；支持证据ID：E2, E4, E10, E13。

## epoch1

Useful cautious synthesis but adds unsupported harm and flattens source dates/types.

优点：

- Avoids inferring HCW protection from antiviral surrogates and appropriately states unquantified potential harms.

具体问题：

- [major] E9concerns possible viral entry through eyes, not equipment injury or spray adverse effects.；支持证据ID：E9。
- [moderate] Includes2022full-text recommendation;in-vitro findings are not simulations. Does not clearly state no completed studies and three ongoing nearly700.；支持证据ID：E2, E4, E13。

## epoch1_5

具体问题：
