## base

Direct and well-calibrated synthesis of the relevant fatigue evidence; limited certainty and modality generalization are preserved.

优点：

- Accurately reports all four low-certainty fatigue SMD/CIs, massage energy effect, HD scope, short follow-up and unresolved drug/PD effects without importing RLS efficacy.

具体问题：

- 本次未记录实质问题。

## epoch1

Generally sound fatigue synthesis with localized contamination from an irrelevant RLS review.

优点：

- Covers all four potentially useful approaches, uncertainty of other treatments and sparse safety/long-term outcomes.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Imports restless-legs exercise comparator into fatigue review and invents injuries. Supplied fatigue exercise comparison is control, not resistance versus no resistance.；支持证据ID：E1, E3, E4。

## epoch1_5

Avoids directly using irrelevant RLS effects, but misses supplied statistics and overstates uncertainty selectively.

优点：

- Separates RLS from fatigue evidence and notes massage result and sparse serious outcomes.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Exercise/aromatherapy SMDs and CIs are supplied. Opening unfairly singles out exercise when massage/acupressure/aromatherapy have the same low-certainty may-improve conclusion.；支持证据ID：E4, E5, E6。

## epoch2

Core intervention results are useful, but repeated false missing-data claims obscure available limitations.

优点：

- Reports exercise/aromatherapy/massage estimates correctly and keeps low certainty and other-treatment uncertainty.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Named fatigue scales, generally short follow-up and rare/inconsistent adverse-event reporting are explicitly supplied; no children were included rather than child status being unreported.；支持证据ID：E4, E5, E6。
