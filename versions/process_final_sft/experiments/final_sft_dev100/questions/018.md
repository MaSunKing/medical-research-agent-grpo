## base

Faithful chronic synthesis but incomplete acute/subacute and treatment-specific harm coverage; this is an evidence-selection limitation, not a length penalty.

优点：

- Accurately identifies chronic CBT/mindfulness, goal setting/needling, lower-certainty exercise/multidisciplinary care, and sham-controlled acupuncture/manipulation findings.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Does not explain immediate-term exercise, heat-wrap and manual-therapy benefit; medication-harm discussion displaces directly relevant no-events-reported for acupuncture/education/exercise/manual therapy and mild heat-wrap harms.；支持证据ID：E5, E10。

## epoch1

Useful breadth and calibration but central acute benefit quantities use incorrect units.

优点：

- Covers acute and chronic options separately and recognizes incomplete harm reporting.

具体问题：

- [major] Converts standardized mean differences to raw scale points without justification.；支持证据ID：E5。
- [minor] The harms passage is full text; no events reported is not a quantified safety comparison.；支持证据ID：E10。

## epoch1_5

Question drift plus invented evidence gaps leave the requested non-drug benefit-harm answer largely unmet.

优点：

- Copies several acute/subacute SMDs accurately.

具体问题：

- [major] Substantial medicine-focused discussion displaces requested treatments, while explicit chronic CBT/mindfulness/disability estimates are denied.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E5。
- [moderate] The estimates are immediate-term; one-trial claim and walking detail are absent.；支持证据ID：E5。

## epoch2

Broad structure cannot compensate for invented effect size, direction confusion and cross-study safety attribution.

优点：

- Lists relevant chronic and acute treatments and notes comparator heterogeneity.

具体问题：

- [major] Invents a raw pain conversion; disability reduction is improved functioning, and repeated wording reverses that interpretation.；支持证据ID：E5, E7。
- [moderate] Transfers acute-study harms to chronic CBT and denies the controlled comparisons specified in E2.；支持证据ID：E2, E10。
