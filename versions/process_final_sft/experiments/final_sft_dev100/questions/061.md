优点：

- Accurate1.5fewer vertigo-days estimate and recognizes low-certainty efficacy,publication bias and local injection/perforation events.

具体问题：

- [major] Silently conflates reviews: E2reportsSAEs3.0%vs4.4%RR0.64CI0.22–1.85very-low certainty,while E4/E9report noSAEswith moderate-certainty safety. Minimal-risk claim unsupported by unresolved conflict.；支持证据ID：E2, E4, E9。

## epoch1

Some useful cautious content but weak quantitative integration and imprecise units/strata.

- Notes modest vertigo improvement and potential local adverse events plus unpublished trials.

具体问题：

- [major] 0.05is proportion, not days;472participants accompanies estimate in source. Does not distinguish newer moderate-certainty safety review from Cochrane very-low-certainty serious events.；支持证据ID：E2, E4。
- [moderate] Omits explicit serious event3.0vs4.4andRR0.64CI0.22–1.85; loses important risk evidence.；支持证据ID：E2。

## epoch1_5
