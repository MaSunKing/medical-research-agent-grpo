## base

Strong focused efficacy answer with appropriate caveats, slightly overstating formal equivalence and broad safety.

优点：

- Integrates similar efficacy, trial hyperglycemia/stay advantages, imprecision and severe-COPD applicability limits accurately.

具体问题：

- [minor] Likely sufficient/similar outcomes is better supported than formal noninferiority or overall safety superiority. Review short courses span3–7days and excluded assisted ventilation, which is omitted.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E5, E7, E8。

## epoch1

Generally useful efficacy comparison, but invented qualifications obscure the review's positive five-day sufficiency conclusion.

优点：

- Accurately tabulates the 170-person trial and recognizes severe-COPD applicability limits.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Duplicates of the same review are treated as separate reviews, and an invented untested-relapse limitation contradicts the explicitly pooled relapse outcome.；支持证据ID：E1, E5。

## epoch1_5

Multiple quantitative and source-attribution errors undermine otherwise relevant cautious advice.

优点：

- Reports treatment-success percentages and ventilated-patient exclusion.

具体问题：

- [major] Trial has 170 participants. Mixes its −0.8-day difference with pooled −0.61-day CI and denies explicit hyperglycemia outcome.；支持证据ID：E1, E7, E8。
- [moderate] E6 is the duration systematic review, not the biomarker narrative review.；支持证据ID：E6。

## epoch2

Main direction is recognizable but numerical misreading and false evidence gaps materially weaken support.

优点：

- Correctly reports treatment-failure OR and imprecision.

具体问题：

- [major] 72 and 70 are counts (84.7% versus 82.4%); relapse is OR, and the numerical adverse effect is hyperglycemia (the source conclusion itself contains a hypoglycemia typo).；支持证据ID：E5, E7, E8。
- [moderate] Ventilated patients were excluded and five-day sufficiency is expressly supplied.；支持证据ID：E2, E4。
