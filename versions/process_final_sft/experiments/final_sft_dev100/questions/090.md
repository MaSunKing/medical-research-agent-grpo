## base

Appropriate overall uncertainty and population scope, but too generic about effectiveness and overreads intervention/follow-up detail.

优点：

- Recognizes small athlete-only biased evidence, no placebo comparison and absent muscle/schedule details.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Function may make little/no difference, not established improvement. E1 defines eligible exercise types rather than proving each was used;12months is surgery-comparison follow-up, not supplied program/trial duration ceiling. Omits outcome-specific comparator results and missing adverse events.；支持证据ID：E1, E3, E6, E7。

## epoch1

Relevant comparison layout but repeated population, number, sign and outcome errors distort the synthesis.

优点：

- Reports no-treatment pain signal and very-low-certainty status; includes surgery pain uncertainty.

具体问题：

- [major] All participants are athletes; injection comparison is6.04points worse with exercise; surgery has one trial,12-month follow-up is supplied, and shoulder satisfaction is invented.；支持证据ID：E3, E7, E8。

## epoch1_5

Broad relevant coverage with several fabricated/confused temporal and statistical interpretations.

优点：

- Reports correct pain/function groups, athlete population, surgery return-to-sport and lack of placebo trials.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Pain/function are distinct outcomes, not contradictory; no-treatment function pools two trials; eight-month trial is not supplied. Injection function numerical direction is not a proven advantage given CI.；支持证据ID：E3, E6, E7, E8。

## epoch2

Central result extraction is materially inaccurate despite recognizing broad uncertainty.

优点：

- Identifies small athlete-only evidence base, bias and absence of specific muscle/protocol details.

具体问题：

- [major] Pain estimate uses one study39participants; function two95 with MD7.04 low certainty, not6.04 or very low; injection comparison is low certainty rather than high/medium. Opening overstates proven functional benefit.；支持证据ID：E3, E6, E8。
