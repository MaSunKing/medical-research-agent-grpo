## base

Mostly supported accessible summary with incomplete service delivery detail and some extrapolation of clinic routines.

优点：

- Integrates visual-acuity/refraction procedures with schools,outreach,vision centres,clinics and spectacle vouchers.

具体问题：

- [moderate] GenericUSclinic descriptions do not establish worldwide norms. Omits pharmacies,home-delivery combinations,payment/free outreach provision and explicitWHOregional patterns despite direct evidence.；支持证据ID：E2, E4, E6, E12, E14。

## epoch1

Reasonable route overview but spectacle provision and worldwide distribution remain incomplete.

优点：

- Clearly distinguishes community routes from fixed clinics and avoids claiming a best global model.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Three resources need not be studies; generic clinic procedure is extrapolated to all routes. Omits payment/vouchers, free outreach spectacles, general health centres and regional differences.；支持证据ID：E2, E4, E6, E12。

## epoch1_5

具体问题：

- [minor] Passage specifies letters and numbers on Snellen chart.；支持证据ID：E13。
