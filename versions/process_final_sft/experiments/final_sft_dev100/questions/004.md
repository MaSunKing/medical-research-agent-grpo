## base

Important comparison errors and Checklist-driven narrowing limit the useful synthesis despite a few correct observations.

优点：

- Correctly identifies the unchanged potassium result of dietary acid-load interventions and the potential RAAS-maintenance role of newer binders.

具体问题：

- [major] E1/E2 explicitly include usual-care controls, E6 includes placebo/standard care randomization, and E5 reports little or no difference in calcium-versus-sodium polystyrene potassium reduction, not superiority.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E5, E6。
- [moderate] Lets a narrower Checklist gap dominate the broader original question and underuses the explicit randomized potassium-lowering finding inE8. Uncertain long-term clinical benefit must not erase biomarker efficacy.；支持证据ID：E4, E8。

## epoch1

Some useful evidence selection, but comparator distortion and unsupported treatment language materially compromise the answer.

优点：

- Distinguishes potassium-lowering binders from dietary manipulation for acidosis, and recognizes limited comparative and clinical-outcome evidence.

具体问题：

- [major] Invents a specific head-to-head result and denies comparator trials that E6 explicitly says included placebo or standard care. Missing quantitative results are not absence of such trials.；支持证据ID：E5, E6。
- [major] Provides a nonsensical treatment implication not supported by the evidence package.；支持证据ID：E5, E8。

## epoch1_5

Extensive detail does not compensate for central drug/comparator and outcome-binding errors.

优点：

- Includes the 15-study population and flags the distinction between binder outcomes and dietary-acidosis studies.

具体问题：

- [major] The numerical comparison is calcium versus sodium polystyrene sulfonate, not patiromer. The later table does not resolve the contradictory drug binding in the main text.；支持证据ID：E5。
- [major] Contradicts the source's clear bicarbonate improvement (MD2.98); only serum potassium was unchanged.；支持证据ID：E1, E3。
- [major] Adds unsupported comparative, protocol and date claims and mixes source scope; incoherent phrasing makes the conditional efficacy conclusion difficult to use.；支持证据ID：E5, E6, E8。

## epoch2

A reasonable opening is contradicted by an invented null result on the question's central outcome.

优点：

- Separates laboratory potassium reduction from long-term clinical outcomes, and correctly notes unchanged potassium in acidosis-focused diet studies.

具体问题：

- [major] Fabricates a pooled neutral potassium result; E6 describes trial design without that estimate, while E8 reports reduction with the newer binders.；支持证据ID：E6, E8。
- [moderate] Invents unreported standard-care subgroups and treats absent subgroup data as a neutral result rather than an unknown.；支持证据ID：E5, E6。
