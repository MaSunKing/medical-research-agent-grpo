## base

Reports source facts but overclaims the testing-specific causal conclusion; cancer-type caveat does not repair comparator confounding.

优点：

- Accurately reports targeted-versus-nontargeted NSCLC OS direction, NGS-versus-routine result and BTC PFS medians.

具体问题：

- [major] Available comparators are routine genetic testing, nontargeted therapy or conventional chemotherapy, not targeted therapy without testing. Observational findings cannot isolate causal testing benefit.；支持证据ID：E1, E2, E4。

## epoch1

Mostly appropriate uncertainty, but key population and comparator misattributions distort the question's answer.

优点：

- Correctly reports NGS one-year survival and lack of later survival advantage; identifies observational limits.

具体问题：

- [major] Progression evidence is BTC, not NSCLC; NSCLC targeted therapy is compared with nontargeted therapy, not conventional targeted therapy.；支持证据ID：E2, E4。

## epoch1_5

Good relevant synthesis overall, with some awkward phrasing and slight population/design imprecision.

优点：

- Separates NGS-versus-routine testing from targeted-versus-nontargeted treatment; gives BTC PFS/OS and observational limitations.

具体问题：

- [minor] Source specifies stage IV and R2 resection, with nonrandom treatment groups; assignment wording could imply allocation.；支持证据ID：E4。
