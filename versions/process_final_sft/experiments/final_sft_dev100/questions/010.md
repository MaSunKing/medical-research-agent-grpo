## base

Accurate and question-focused use of the central progression results; material scope/safety details are incomplete, not fabricated.

优点：

- Answers progression in establishedAMD, correctly differentiates early/intermediate absolute benefits and intervals, preserves moderate certainty and acknowledges safety limitations without inventing numbers.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Useful but generic: the supplied beta-carotene lung-cancer warning in people who have smoked is an important concrete safety qualification that is omitted.；支持证据ID：E5, E9。
- [minor] Could more explicitly identify combination-formula evidence rather than imply equal benefit from every individual supplement; vitaminE-alone results are uncertain.；支持证据ID：E5, E6。

## epoch1

Useful main effect estimates, but an unnecessarily confused conflict section and scope transfers weaken otherwise relevant synthesis.

优点：

- Answers progression rather than primary prevention, reports the correctOR0.72 and early/intermediate absolute benefits, and mentions beta-carotene safety.

具体问题：

- [moderate] The six-month-to-five-year range describes the lutein comparisons, not the principal pooled progression trials. The supplied quality-of-life result is not specifically established for the early-AMD subgroup.；支持证据ID：E6。
- [moderate] Manufactures a conflict and specific early-AMD comparator not supplied; the prevention and progression reviews address different populations and are not contradictory.；支持证据ID：E6, E9。

## epoch1_5

The strongest endpoint qualification of the three, but still contains subgroup and supplement-attribution errors.

优点：

- Distinguishes established disease from prevention, gives the correct progression figures, and explicitly recognizes the geographic-atrophyCI crossing1.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Restricts the pooled population to early AMD despite early/intermediate scope, and the paragraph structure incorrectly attributes the quality-of-life evidence to vitaminE alone.；支持证据ID：E4, E6。
- [moderate] E10 explicitly reports progression and vision-loss benefit for the combination and identifies its comparisons. Combining this with the primary-prevention review creates a misleading account of the older evidence.；支持证据ID：E10, E11。

## epoch2

The correct headline is diluted by a false comparator and several contradictions of the supplied passages.

优点：

- Correctly gives the pooled progression effect and different absolute benefits by disease stage, and cautions against assuming supplements are harmless.

具体问题：

- [major] Replaces placebo/no-intervention with a diagnosis-timing comparator, changing what the trials tested.；支持证据ID：E3, E4。
- [moderate] The geographic-atrophyCI includes no difference; E6 supplies numerical exploratory lutein-replacementHRs, and E10 explicitly describes people with signs of disease. These statements overclaim one endpoint while falsely declaring other supplied evidence absent.；支持证据ID：E5, E6, E10。
