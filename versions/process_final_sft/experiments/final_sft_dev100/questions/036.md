## base

Mostly accurate and cautious summary,but initial route/population generalization is broader than supplied results.

优点：

- Correct positive intrauterine pregnancy/live-birth trend,variable certainty,absent ovarian-specific evidence and need for better trials/protocols.

具体问题：

- [moderate] Opening extends potential efficacy to ovarian route before later retracting it;should immediately restrict observed benefit to intrauterinePRP,especially repeated transfer-failure populations. Thin-endometrium subgroup exception is omitted.；支持证据ID：E1, E6, E7, E8, E11。

## epoch1

Route distinction is useful, but fabricated absolute pregnancy rates and incorrect effect metric materially distort efficacy.

优点：

- Correctly distinguishes intrauterine evidence from absent ovarian-specific pregnancy outcomes.

具体问题：

- [major] E6 isRR notOR; absolute rates are invented. Umbrella review reports increased live births in most comparisons, usually low certainty; should not deny this positive evidence.；支持证据ID：E6, E7, E10。
